36	How we make our products
37	We have made everything we sell at our own factory in Shihor since we started. That lets us control what goes into each batch.
38	Seaweed and humic substances are extracted in-house by a mild process, without harsh chemicals, very high pH or heat.
39	Liquid capacity is 100 KL a month and solid and granule capacity is 2,500 t a month, so dealer and bulk orders come from the same line as farmer packs.
40	The same team that makes the formulas walks farmers' fields, so problems we see in the field go back into the formulas.
41	See <a href="/factory-and-rd">factory and R&amp;D</a> for the plant and <a href="/quality">quality and approvals</a> for the NOCs and batch checks.
