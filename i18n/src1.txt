253	Apple stage by stage
254	Apple growers in the hills face trees dying in the orchard, low production and poor fruit, while costs rise every year. Many of these problems start in the root zone and build up over seasons.
255	Main growing areas: Himachal Pradesh and Jammu and Kashmir.
256	Dormancy
257	Winter
258	Pruning, orchard cleaning and soil work. Chilling hours decide how well buds break in spring.
259	Bud break and flowering
260	Spring
261	Even bud break and good pollination set the crop. Frost and poor pollination reduce fruit set.
262	Fruit growth
263	Summer
264	Fruit size, colour and firmness build up. Water, balanced nutrition and leaf health matter most.
265	After harvest
266	Autumn
267	The tree stores food for next year. Leaf health after harvest matters for next season's buds.
268	Common apple problems
269	Problems farmers bring to us
270	Trees dying in the orchard
271	Root rot and collar rot
272	Low production
273	Small, poorly coloured fruit
274	Rising input costs
275	What to check first
276	Dig near a weak tree and look at the roots: healthy roots are white and firm, rotting roots are brown and soft.
277	Check drainage: water standing around trunks invites root rot.
278	Look at the collar (where trunk meets soil) for cankers.
279	Test the soil for pH and organic carbon.

330	Banana stage by stage
331	Banana grows fast and feeds heavily. In Central Gujarat growers brought us wilt and leaf spot problems, plant losses and poor bunch weight. Soil health, clean planting material and balanced nutrition all matter.
332	Main growing areas: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka.
333	Planting and establishment
334	About the first 2 to 3 months
335	Disease-free suckers or tissue-culture plants and good drainage set the plantation up.
336	Vegetative growth
337	About 3 to 7 months
338	The plant builds leaves; the number of healthy leaves decides bunch size later.
339	Shooting and bunch
340	About 7 to 10 months
341	The bunch emerges and fills. Healthy leaves, water and potash matter most.
342	Harvest
343	About 11 to 14 months
344	Bunch weight and finger quality depend on how the plant was fed all season.
345	Common banana problems
346	Wilt (Panama disease)
347	Leaf spot (Sigatoka)
348	Plants dying
349	Low bunch weight
350	Poor finger size and quality
351	Use disease-free planting material.
352	Look for yellowing that starts on older leaves and a split, discoloured stem, which point to wilt.
353	Remove badly spotted leaves.
354	Keep drainage good; banana hates standing water.

408	About 2 to 3 months

415	Yellowing
416	Zinc shortage
417	Poor grain filling
