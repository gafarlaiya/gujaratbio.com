83	Five checks before you buy any biostimulant
84	Ask for the Schedule VI grade name, exactly as it is written in the Fertiliser Control Order.
85	Check the label for the manufacturer's name and address, batch number, and manufacturing and expiry dates.
86	Ask for proof of the approval or certificate behind the product.
87	Be careful with labels that promise a fixed yield increase or say the product 'cures' disease.
88	Buy from a seller who will still answer your call after the sale.

111	Why gentle extraction matters
112	Seaweed, humic and amino acid extracts carry natural compounds that can break down under very strong alkali, acid or high heat. Harsh processing is faster and cheaper, but it can leave a dark liquid with less of what the plant actually responds to. We extract without harsh chemicals, very high pH or heat, which takes longer but keeps more of the raw material's value.
